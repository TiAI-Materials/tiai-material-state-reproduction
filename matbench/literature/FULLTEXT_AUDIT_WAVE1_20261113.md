## Immediate literature-grounded decision

**Do not present a universal protocol/federation or mixed-agent leaderboard as first-of-kind.** General Agent Evaluation / Exgentic already built a native-protocol adaptation layer and compared multiple agents/models/benchmarks. OccuBench covers cross-industry work in *simulated* environments; CUA-Recovery already audits inherited/policy-induced error recovery. The testable remaining distinction for TiAI is the **combination** of task-level independent native verifier, scientific state/provenance and unit/phase/scale semantics, complete intervention and cost accounting, and matched recoverability/false-completion effects. This remains a research hypothesis until the frozen six-arm runs actually execute.

**Direct reusable patterns:** Exgentic black-box wrappers (subject to version/license check), TheAgentCompany checkpoint/finalization model, LongDS state rollback labels, HORIZON failure taxonomy, CUA-Recovery inherited-error depth, OccuBench implicit missing-data injection, and RoadmapBench target-level partial scoring. Reuse as modular adapters or diagnostics, not by reimplementing other authors' complete environments.

**Material vs simulated evidence boundary:** language-environment simulators, synthetic scorer fixtures and generated tasks do not certify scientific result correctness or laboratory physical safety. Physical robot/instrument claims require separate supervised/native evidence.
